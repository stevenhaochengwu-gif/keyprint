TypedMistakes

Analysis

Overview

Every test you finish keeps each key you pressed, so this page can show where your mistakes come from. A key counts as missed when your first try at it was wrong.

Practice these

A test made of words full of your most missed keys and trickiest key switches, picked from the cards below. It changes as you improve.

Most missed keys

How often each key goes wrong when it's the one to type, most missed first. Spaces, numbers and symbols count too. A key you've typed only a few times ranks a little lower until there's more to go on.

What you type instead

The key you meant, then the key you hit, most common first. Each one has the likely reason, worked out from your layout and fingers.

Tricky key switches

Many mistakes start in the move from one key to the next. These are the switches that go wrong more often than the key they land on usually does, with how your fingers travel for each.

Shift and combinations

Capitals and shifted symbols take two keys at once. Here's how they compare with keys that need no Shift, and which Shift slips you make.

Punctuation and symbols

Every mark, number and symbol you've typed, with how often it goes wrong. Pick more of them under Marks to include in Settings to practice them.

Fingers and hands

Mistakes by the finger that should press each key, from your keyboard layout and finger pattern in Settings.

Slow switches

The switches where you hesitate, by your typical time from the first key to the second. Mistakes and long pauses are left out.

Rushing

How often a key goes wrong at different speeds. Each key is measured against your usual time between keys in the same test.

Where in a word

Mistakes by where the key sits in its word. A high first letter usually means the jump from the space bar into the next word.

Recent mistakes

Your latest slips, newest first, each in the word where it happened.
